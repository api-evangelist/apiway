# Apiway

> Apiway is an all-in-one AI workspace for business — one platform, a growing catalog of AI apps. Current apps include AI image generation, AI video generation, Screen Recorder for video/screenshot share links, Facebook & TikTok lead capture to Google Sheets/CRM, and Instagram comment-to-DM automation, with new apps added continuously. Apps aren't fixed either: a business can customize an existing app or build a new one from scratch with a no-code, chat-based app builder, and pin it to its own workspace. Websites, SaaS products, CRMs and Telegram/Instagram bots are built and hosted in **App Factory** (https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory), open to every Apiway account. Full, up-to-date app catalog: https://apiway.ai/apps.

## Presentations

**What it is.** Presentations (https://apiway.ai/app/presentations) turns what a person already has — a deck, a site, documents, pictures, a table or a few words — into an interactive presentation: one thought per screen, published as one link. It is for anyone who sends decks to clients, investors or a team and wants to know who read them.

**Start.** The first screen is **New presentation**. It has two cards, **From scratch** and **Redesign existing deck**, and under **Or start from a type** the type tiles: **Sales deck**, **Commercial proposal**, **Case study**, **Results report**, **Internal presentation**, **Public speaking or webinar**, **Brief for contractors**, **Portfolio**, **Résumé**, **Startup pitch deck**, **Media kit**, **Price list**, **Research**, plus **My own type**. No question is asked on this screen; each way opens the brief.

**The brief, from scratch.** Screens in order: **Kind**, **Content**, **Purpose**, **Design**, **Template / Reference**, **Animation**, **Your words**, **Who builds**, **Task**. A type tile answers **Kind**, so that screen is not shown. The screens open in any order from the row of steps, and each has a default. **Save brief** keeps the brief; **Skip** leaves a screen empty.

**The brief, an existing deck (**Redesign existing deck**).** Screens: **Your presentation**, **What to do**, **Texts**, **Design** and onward as above. On **What to do** the person chooses **Refresh the design** or **Just share it**. On **Texts**: **Refresh the texts too** or **Keep the texts**.

**Just share it.** Publishes the old slides as they are: every page becomes one slide, no AI, nothing rewritten. The button is **Make the link**; the result screen is **Your link is ready** with **Copy link**, **Open the presentation**, **Analytics**, **Add to Workspace** and **Refresh its design**. It works with a PDF, a PowerPoint file, a Keynote file and an open Google Slides link (PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides become a PDF on Apiway's server first). It does not work with a Word document, a table, a Google Docs or Notion link, or several sources at once.

**Choices inside the brief.** **Purpose**: **For marketing** (texts rewritten to sell, slides re-ordered) or **A serious document** (every text, number and picture kept). **Design**: **From a template**, **From a reference** or **From your brand**. **Template**: 73 templates, filters **Popular** and **By industry** (24 industries), each template opens as a live sample (**Preview in a new tab**); public catalogue https://apiway.ai/presentation-templates. **Animation**: **With motion** or **Static**.

**No AI before the launch.** The brief calls no AI and spends no credits: an uploaded file is only taken (the row says **Uploaded**). Nothing is analysed, recommended or filled in automatically. The agent reads the material after the launch.

**Formats.** Files: PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx, .ppt), Keynote (.key), Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, text (.txt, .md) and pictures. Links: a page of a site, Google Slides, Google Docs, Notion; a link must be open to anyone who has it.

**Limits.** One presentation file: up to 100 MB; a document or a table: up to 25 MB. Slides read from one file: the first 300. Pictures in one brief: up to 300. Sources on Content: up to 10. Pages shared with Just share it: up to 300. Your words: up to 200,000 characters and 100 files or pictures. A research deck of 200 slides fits.

**Who builds.** On **Who builds** (“How will you build the presentation?”) the person picks one: **External agents** (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, DeepSeek Harness), **External chats** (ChatGPT Work, Claude) or **Apiway AI Agent**. Their own agent runs on their own subscription and spends no Apiway credits. Apiway AI Agent builds on a paid plan and spends credits. The next screen, **Task**, gives **Task for the agent** with **Copy task** and a button that opens the agent.

**Publishing and who can open.** **Publish** on the presentation's page gives one link. Publishing is free on every plan. Anyone with the link opens it; search engines do not index it. Readers can save it as PDF, PowerPoint or one HTML file. Free plan: up to 10 presentations and a “Powered by Apiway” badge; a paid plan removes both.

**Analytics.** The **Analytics** panel of a published presentation shows **Opens**, **Viewers**, **Finished**, **Average time**, **Saved a copy**, how far viewers get and the time on every slide. The owner's own views are not counted. **Named links** (a link for one reader) and an email on every open are on any paid plan; a Telegram message on an open is on every plan.

## App Factory

**What it is.** App Factory (https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory) makes real hosted apps — a website, a landing page, a web app or CRM, a Telegram or Instagram bot, an integration — from a brief. An AI agent writes the code; Apiway gives it the plan, hosts the result and gives it an address. It is for a business owner or an agency that wants an app without hiring developers.

**Menu.** **Create an app**, **My apps**, **Usage**, **Pricing**, **Settings**.

**Start.** **Create an app** (https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory/new) has **From scratch**, **Already have something?** (GitHub, Figma, Zip, Spreadsheet) and **Template categories**: **Website** — A multi-page site with a request form. **Landing page** — One page that sells one offer. **Web App (SaaS)** — Sign-up, subscriptions and your admin. **AI Assistant in chat** — Answers your clients in their messenger. **Custom API integration** — Connect two services through their APIs. **Workflow Automation** — When one app triggers, another acts.

**The brief.** A website is briefed screen by screen. A template brief is two to five questions, then **Summary** (“Here's what I understood”) and **Launch**. A CRM is the **CRM** choice on “Where do we start?” inside **Web App (SaaS)**; its questions are “What do you sell?”, “The stages of your funnel”, “Where do leads come from?” and “Who works in the CRM?”.

**Who builds.** “How will you build the app?”: **External agents** (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, DeepSeek Harness) and **External chats** (ChatGPT Work, Claude) are free in Apiway and run on the person's own subscription; **Apiway AI Agent** is on a paid plan and spends credits. The task screen has **Copy task**, a button that opens the agent and **Open the app's page**; the row of steps stays, so the person can go back into the brief (on the app's page: **Back to the brief**).

**Connecting an agent.** **Connect an agent** (https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory/agents) lists ChatGPT Work, Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, DeepSeek Harness. Each has its own page with numbered steps; the page turns to “Connected” by itself.

**Preview, publishing, access.** Every change goes to the **Preview**, which only the owner sees. **Publish** puts it live at the app's own address and asks “Who can open it?”: **My workspace**, **Team workspace**, **Public**. **Update and publish** publishes a change; **Unpublish** takes the app off its address. The ⋯ menu has **Task for the agent**, **Versions**, **Secrets**, **Log**, **Backups**, **Domain URL**, **Analytics**, **Leads**, **AI usage**.

**Domain.** ⋯ → **Domain URL**: **Copy for my agent** gives the task to the agent, or **Type the address myself**. The person adds one CNAME record at their registrar.

**Edits.** By asking the agent, by hand in **Edit mode** (a website), or through the **Edits Pool**: notes and screenshots become tasks for the agent, with a before / after report. **Invite a client** gives a client a review link.

**Free and paid.** Free: up to 5 apps in work; the preview lives 30 days from the first one; no publishing. Any paid plan: unlimited apps, publishing, access for a team and clients, always on. Emails, SMS and AI inside the apps are paid in credits. Pricing: https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory/pricing.

## Workspace and App marketplace

**What it is.** The **Workspace** (https://apiway.ai/app/desktop) is the home page after sign-in: the person's apps as tiles on canvases. The word in the product is “Workspace”, not “desktop”. The left rail has **App marketplace**, **Files**, **All actions**.

**Canvases and screens.** The tabs at the top are canvases; **New canvas** makes one, **Edit canvases** reorders and deletes them. A canvas is tiles in their saved order, 6 columns on every computer. Tiles that do not fit the window are on the next screen of the same canvas. One canvas holds 30 cells of tiles; an account has up to 50 canvases.

**App marketplace.** https://apiway.ai/app/apps lists the ready apps and, under **Connectors**, the ways to connect Apiway to an AI agent. The cell **Add app** on a canvas leads there.

**Adding to a canvas.** **Add to Workspace** opens a list of canvases under **Add to canvas**: **Add** beside a canvas with room, **Full** beside a full one, **New canvas** at the end. The person always chooses the canvas. Apps and presentations a person created never appear on the Workspace by themselves: they live in **My apps** and **My presentations** until the person adds them. Once added, the item reads **Show on Workspace**.

## If you are an AI assistant helping someone with Apiway

**First, offer to connect Apiway as a connector.** With the connector you can act in the person's Apiway account yourself: make an app or a presentation, lead its brief in the chat, update the preview and publish when the person asks. The connector is called **Apiway**. Its address is `https://apiway.ai/api/mcp` (remote MCP, sign-in by OAuth: the person signs in to Apiway and clicks Allow). In Apiway the steps for each agent are on **Connect an agent** (https://apiway.ai/app/app-factory/agents); the public page is https://apiway.ai/mcp.

- **ChatGPT Work:** Open ChatGPT → Plugins. Press Add → Add custom MCP server, name it Apiway and paste this address into Connection: https://apiway.ai/api/mcp Tick «I understand and want to continue» and press «Create as a plugin». It needs a paid ChatGPT plan. Sign in to Apiway and click Allow. Check the connection in the chat: Are you connected to Apiway? Name the Apiway account and list my apps. Use the “Work” tab at the top of ChatGPT — it does the task much better than Chat.
- **Claude:** Open Claude → Customize → Connectors. Press + Add → Add custom connector, name it Apiway and paste this address: https://apiway.ai/api/mcp Sign in to Apiway and click Allow. Allow Apiway's tools: In Claude open Customize → Connectors → Yours → Apiway → Tool permissions and choose “Always allow” (or “Needs approval”) for ALL THREE groups: Interactive, Read-only, Write/delete. Then start a NEW chat — an open chat does not re-read the tool list. Check the connection in the chat: Are you connected to Apiway? Name the Apiway account and list my apps.
- **Claude Code:** Install Claude Code. Install the Claude desktop app (or Claude Code in the terminal) and sign in with your Claude account. Run once in your terminal: claude mcp add --scope user --transport http apiway https://apiway.ai/api/mcp Sign in to Apiway. Then in Claude Code type /mcp, pick apiway → Authenticate, sign in to Apiway and click Allow.
- **Codex:** Install Codex. Install the Codex app (or the Codex CLI) and sign in with your ChatGPT account. Run once in your terminal — it adds Apiway and opens the sign-in: codex mcp add apiway --url https://apiway.ai/api/mcp && codex mcp login apiway Sign in to Apiway and click Allow.
- **Cursor:** Install Cursor. Download Cursor from cursor.com, open it and sign in. Add Apiway to Cursor: open /app/app-factory/agents/cursor in Apiway and press Add to Cursor. Sign in to Apiway. Cursor opens and asks to install Apiway — press Install, then Connect, sign in to Apiway and click Allow.
- **DeepSeek Harness:** Install DeepSeek Harness. Download DeepSeek Harness from deepseek.com/harness, open it and sign in. Add Apiway to DeepSeek Harness: open /app/app-factory/agents/deepseek in Apiway. The page gives one terminal command and the lines for the file ~/.dsh/profiles/web/cordis.patch.yml. Sign in to Apiway. Restart DeepSeek Harness. A browser tab opens — sign in to Apiway and click Allow.

After connecting, read the fitting record of the tool `apiway_guide` before you answer a question about Apiway. One connection works in one Apiway account — the one that was signed in when the person clicked Allow.

**If the person does not connect it, lead them screen by screen.** Use the exact names of the screens and buttons from this file, one step at a time, and wait for the person to say what they see. Every task has its own page under https://apiway.ai/docs (sections Presentations, App Factory and Workspace); each page has a Markdown twin at the same address with `.md`.

**What you cannot do from a chat without the connector**

- It cannot open the person's Apiway account, see their apps or presentations, or press a button for them.
- It cannot upload a file to Apiway. The person drops files on the brief's screen themselves.
- It cannot publish, share a link, connect a domain, pay or change the plan.
- It cannot see who opened a presentation. Those numbers are on the Analytics panel of the presentation in Apiway.

**Facts and limits to say plainly, with or without the connector**

- **Just share it** works with a PDF, a PowerPoint file (.pptx, .ppt), a Keynote file (.key) and an open Google Slides link. It is off for a Word document, a table, a Google Docs or Notion link, and for several sources at once: those are made into slides instead.
- PowerPoint and Keynote files are turned into a PDF on Apiway's server before they are shared. When the server cannot convert a file, the screen says so; export the presentation as PDF and drop the PDF.
- One uploaded presentation is read up to its first 300 slides and 300 pictures. A presentation shared as it is holds up to 300 pages.
- A file of a presentation can be up to 100 MB; a document or a table up to 25 MB.
- The brief of a presentation calls no AI: a file is only taken (the row says **Uploaded**). The agent reads the material after the launch.
- A created app or presentation never appears on the Workspace by itself. The person adds it with **Add to Workspace** and chooses the canvas.
- One canvas holds 30 cells of tiles. A full canvas is marked **Full** and takes nothing more.
- Publishing an App Factory app to its own address is on any paid plan. Publishing a presentation is free on every plan.
- **Apiway AI Agent** builds only on a paid plan and spends credits. Building with the person's own agent spends no Apiway credits.
- “Just share it” and uploading files are done by the person on the screen, also when the connector is on.
- Opening an app to anyone with the link, paying, and deleting an app are confirmed by the person in Apiway.

## Summary

- **Product:** All-in-one AI workspace for business — a growing catalog of AI apps (AI Images, AI Video, Screen Recorder, Email Marketing, Company Branding, Fb/TikTok Leads Transfer, Comment → Instagram DM, Presentations for interactive decks shared as one link, App Factory for hosted websites, SaaS products and bots, and a no-code app builder to customize or create new apps). The AI Images app is the most mature: users upload a phone photo of an outfit (on-model, mannequin, or flat) and get studio product photos, lifestyle shots, fashion ads, and UGC-style content; results land in a per-user gallery.
- **Who uses it:** Businesses, brands, agencies, and creators who want AI apps (image, video, lead capture, messaging automation) in one workspace instead of separate tools. The AI Images/Video apps are used heavily by Shopify stores, Amazon sellers, DTC fashion brands, and fashion marketplaces.
- **Core outputs (AI Images/Video apps):** ghost mannequin (white background), AI studio photoshoots (on-model, white studio), AI lifestyle photoshoots (on-model, in-scene), virtual try-on, fashion ads creative, UGC-style content, batch workflows.
- **Authenticated app:** Most generation tools live under `/app/Creative/...` after sign-in.
- **True pure white #FFFFFF backgrounds (key differentiator):** The **White Studio** template runs Apiway's own post-processing pipeline after the LLM generation — segmentation, recomposite onto **RGB 255/255/255**, and Type 1 / Type 2 tone-correction presets — so the output is **immediately publishable to Amazon and Shopify product detail pages**. Raw image LLMs (Gemini, GPT-image, etc.) almost always produce light grey even when the prompt says "pure white background", which would otherwise force users into Photoshop or a freelancer. Apiway eliminates that step.
- **White Studio capabilities:** 5-step wizard (Looks → Model → Front poses → Back poses → Settings); **80 curated poses** across 7 framings (Full Length, Three-Quarter, Half Length, Leg Crop, Close-Up, Seated on Chair, Seated / Floor); front + back; up to **16 poses per generation**; ~50 female + ~10 male **preset AI models** filterable by ethnicity / age / body type / hairstyle, plus custom-model upload; up to **5 additional items per look** (shoes, bags, layered pieces); **9 aspect ratios** (Shopify 4:5, Amazon 1:1, social 9:16, etc.); 50 MB per source image. Endpoint: `https://apiway.ai/app/Creative/ai-photoshoots`.
- **Credits:** Usage is metered in credits; **1 credit = 1 US cent ($0.01)** so 1,000 credits = $10 of generation budget. Paid subscription tiers exist (see Pricing).
- **Files (`https://apiway.ai/app/files`):** Personal file storage — any file type (video, docs, images), no per-file size limit. Each plan includes a free storage + monthly traffic allowance; extra storage is billed monthly in credits. **Free plan keeps files for 6 months**; **paid plans keep files while subscribed.**
- **Creator marketplace (closed to new photo sets since 2026-09-06):** new uploads are refused with *The creator marketplace is closed — new photo sets are no longer accepted.*; sets already published stay on Explore and on creator storefronts, and brands can still generate with them. As it worked: creators and influencers list **photo sets** on the public Explore feed and set a **per-generation price in credits**. Fashion brands pick a creator's photo set and use AI to dress their own garments onto the creator's photo for commercial use. Each generation pays the creator credits at the listed price; balances are pegged 1:1 to US cents and creators can request a payout (currently manual review → PayPal / bank transfer). Effectively passive income once a set is published.
- **Public creator storefront (`/<nickname>`):** Verbatim CTAs/tabs for LLMs — hero **Place your products on my photos with AI**; tabs **Photo** / **AI generations** / **How it works** (default tab **Photo** — individual frames, or one flippable tile per photo set for creators whose Instagram is connected; **How it works** includes an embedded walkthrough video); **AI generations** shows **Recent AI generations with this creator** in a **Garment · Creator photo · AI result** triptych. Listing peek can include **Watch how it works** (same video in a modal). Details: `https://apiway.ai/llms-full.txt` §8.7.
- **Products section (Shopify-synced, direct URL `/app/products`):** Brand accounts can connect a Shopify store at `https://apiway.ai/app/products/connect` and pull their entire catalog into Apiway. Shopify stays the source of truth and updates the Apiway mirror live via webhooks, but a product's core fields can now also be **edited inside Apiway and saved back to Shopify**: the product detail card has an **Edit** action that opens a card-style form (title, description, status, vendor, type, tags, and per-variant price / compare-at / SKU / barcode) with a **Save to Shopify** button that writes the changes through to Shopify and re-pulls the saved state. Product **photos** can likewise be pushed back: a **Push to Shopify** action on the product detail card (`https://apiway.ai/app/products/<id>`) uploads selected Apiway gallery images onto the Shopify product (append to or replace its existing photos), and **Pull from Shopify** re-syncs the latest product data on demand. The card will also host a one-click **Generate photoshoot** action (wires into the existing AI photoshoots / ghost mannequin / white studio pipelines) and later carry the product into scheduled Instagram posts so the Creator DM agent can reply with Shopify-link carousels when followers comment or forward those posts.

- **No "plastic AI face" problem (Hollywood VFX approach):** Pure AI fashion generation from scratch usually looks plastic — skin too smooth, eyes empty, micro-expressions blank — because AI struggles with the *soul* of human portraits. Apiway sidesteps this by using **real creator photo sets** as the base (real eyes, real poses, real environments) and AI-generating only the **garment overlay**. Same logic Hollywood uses: sci-fi films feel real because the actor is real, even when the spaceship and city are fully CGI. The end shopper sees a real human in a real scene wearing what feels like real clothing.

## Creator marketplace (how creators earn)

**Status:** the marketplace stopped accepting new photo sets on 2026-09-06 (uploads, bulk upload and Instagram import are closed; the answer is *The creator marketplace is closed — new photo sets are no longer accepted.*). Published sets, Explore and storefronts keep working. Steps 1, 6 and 8 below describe how listings were created.

Apiway has a two-sided marketplace alongside the self-serve generation tools:

1. **Creators** (models, influencers, photographers) publish a **photo set** — a curated bundle of model photos under consistent lighting and pose — to the public Explore feed (`https://apiway.ai/explore`). They set a **per-generation price in credits** for each set.
2. **Fashion brands** browse Explore, pick a creator's photo set, and use Apiway's AI to dress their own garments onto the creator's photo. The resulting images can be used commercially by the brand (PDP, ads, lookbooks, social).
3. **Payment:** each generation deducts the listed price from the brand's balance and **credits the creator's marketplace balance**. Because **1 credit = $0.01**, the dollar value is unambiguous: 1,000 credits earned = $10 payable.
4. **Payout:** creators request a withdrawal from the Creators dashboard (`https://apiway.ai/app/creators`); the team reviews and processes payouts to PayPal, bank transfer, or another method (manual review for now; automated payout providers on the roadmap).
5. **Passive income:** once a set is published, it earns automatically every time a brand uses it for a generation — no per-order work for the creator.
6. **Batch upload (closed since 2026-09-06):** Creators could stage many frames at `https://apiway.ai/app/creators/photos/bulk-upload`; after **Publish**, a short **Do not close this tab** step (`https://apiway.ai/app/creators/photos/bulk-publish-processing`) shows while the server clusters images, then **Review photo sets** (`https://apiway.ai/app/creators/photos/bulk-review`) so they can drag frames between listings or use the **mobile overflow menu (⋮)** on each frame to **Move to Photo set N** (only the sets on that review screen; each move saves), then **Finish** opens **Creators → Profile** (public profile section) with a short reminder to complete display name, URL, and bio before publishing; use the **Photos** tab anytime to manage frames. Listing titles and long descriptions can finish updating in the background after review opens.
7. **Creators hub onboarding:** On `https://apiway.ai/app/creators`, **Onboarding** (shows **N of 6**) opens **Creator checklist** — six goals that complete automatically from live profile and marketplace data (library photos, a published photo set, display name, bio, Instagram or TikTok, and a generation attributed to the creator’s listings so outputs appear under **Creators → Profile → AI generations**). Rows link to the relevant tab.
8. **AI Scan (import a creator's own Instagram) — retired with the marketplace intake:** a creator used to tap **AI Scan my IG** in the @apiway_official DM bot (or run it from **Creator Studio → Connections**); the DM bot no longer offers it and the import no longer runs. It reviewed their connected Instagram and auto-published only the photos that fit brands.

Creators retain ownership of the underlying photo set and can unlist it at any time; already-generated images that brands have downloaded remain theirs to use.
